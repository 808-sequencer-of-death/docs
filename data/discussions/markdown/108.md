## pjavan — [2026-01-07T20:21:16Z](https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/108#discussioncomment-15438092)

In terms of training there isn't much.  FedRAMP does have an archived repo with an example [SSP](https://github.com/GSA/fedramp-automation/blob/master/src/content/rev5/templates/ssp/xml/FedRAMP-SSP-OSCAL-Template.xml) and [POAM](https://github.com/GSA/fedramp-automation/blob/master/src/content/rev5/templates/poam/xml/FedRAMP-POAM-OSCAL-Template.xml) to give you an idea.    

Can you produce your SSP in Excel?  If so, there are folks that can help you with converting Excel to OSCAL along with commercial tools that enable you to do it yourself.

Reach out to the [OSCAL Foundation](https://oscalfoundation.org/contact-us/) and they may be able to help point you in the right direction.

On the FedRAMP side, we're expecting industry to fill the gap here. This will require concerted effort for each provider along with collaboration with each other and likely support from industry groups and other collaborative alliances. There are a lot of webinars, youtube videos, and blog posts about how folks have attacked this in the past and I'm sure there's more to come, but you'll definitely need to dig in a bit.

I'd also echo pjavan's suggestion that the [OSCAL Foundation](https://oscalfoundation.org/) is a great place to start connecting with one of those industry alliances that has already existed for a minute.

**I hope other folks will chime in here with more resources/thoughts/etc. too!**
