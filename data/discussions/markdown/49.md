We are announcing the final submission of our FedRAMP 20x pilot package for the Meridian Knowledge Solutions Learning Management System. Our 3PAO (Fortreum) has completed their assessment, and we are ready for agency review.

### Key Innovation Highlights

Real-Time Compliance Transparency

**Live Trust Center:** [Public Dashboard](https://meridian-knowledge-solutions.github.io/fedramp_20x_public_submission_live/)
**Automated KSI Validation:** Daily CLI-based evidence collection with machine-readable results
**Complete Audit Trail:** Git-tracked evidence with SHA-based integrity verification

Machine-Readable Compliance Data

**Unified JSON:** All 51 KSI validations with CLI commands, outputs, and assertion logic
**GitHub Actions Pipeline:** Fully automated evidence collection and validation
**Continuous Monitoring:** Real-time compliance tracking with immediate visibility into changes

Community-Driven Transparency

**Open Source Methodology:** Public repository with complete validation logic
**GitHub Integration:** Direct issue reporting for failed KSIs
**Auto-Generated Documentation:** Rule-by-rule explanation of validation criteriaKey Innovation Highlights

### Why This Matters for FedRAMP 20x
This submission demonstrates what we believe to be the practical implementation of GSA's vision for:

- **Automated Assessment:** Machine-readable evidence that reduces manual review burden
- **Continuous Compliance:** Real-time monitoring instead of point-in-time assessments
- **Transparent Trust Models:** Public visibility into compliance status for agency confidence
- **Community Innovation:** Open-source methodology that other CSPs can adopt and improve

### Commitment to Excellence

We are committed to achieving compliance through:

- **Rapid Remediation:** Automated identification and tracking of failed KSIs
- **Continuous Improvement:** Daily validation runs with trend monitoring
- **Iterative Enhancement:** Regular updates based on 3PAO feedback and community input
- **Security-First:** Proactive monitoring and immediate incident response


Ready for agency adoption and community feedback.

Questions about our methodology or technical implementation? Feel free to open an issue in our repository or reach out directly.
