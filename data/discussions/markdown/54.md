# Cloud Security Alliance FedRAMP 20x Pilots
Source: https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/54

## Original post — csa-lhughes, 2025-07-02T16:24:17Z


We are writing to let everyone know that the [Cloud Security Alliance](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/) (CSA) is working on several proof-of-concept pilots to demonstrate that a CSP that meets our Security, Trust, Assurance and Risk (CSA STAR) continuous compliance and automation criteria can qualify for a FedRAMP Low Baseline ATO. 

[CSA STAR](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/star) is a comprehensive program that can provide foundations of any compliance program, including FedRAMP 20x. It encompasses the key principles of transparency, rigorous auditing, and harmonization of standards as put forth in the CSA Cloud Controls Matrix (CCM), and provides a detailed and actionable security framework explicitly designed for cloud environments. More than 300 CSPs have undergone thorough STAR third-party assessments of continuous compliance and automation. Many STAR-certified CSPs have Moderate and High Baseline FedRAMP ATOs, and many STAR third-party assessors are authorized FedRAMP 3PAOs.

[CSA CCM](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/cloud-controls-matrix) is a catalog of 197 control objectives structured in 17 domains covering all key aspects of cloud technology. It is used as a tool for the systematic assessment of a cloud implementation and provides guidance on security controls that should be implemented by a CSP. CCM maps to many other recognized standards – including SOC 2, ISO 27001, NIST 800-53, and FedRAMP itself – and is updated regularly to address emerging threats and changes to regulations. CSA has mapped CCM controls to the FedRAMP 20x KSIs to help demonstrate pilot feasibility.

Integrating STAR and CCM into FedRAMP 20x delivers measurable strategic advantages:

•       Reduced Time-to-Authorization: By aligning FedRAMP processes with CCM’s clearly defined controls, authorization timelines could be reduced significantly—from several months to just weeks.
•       Cost Reduction: Standardization reduces the complexity and redundancy in assessments, resulting in lower costs for CSPs, thereby attracting more providers and increasing marketplace competition.
•       Better Risk Management: Transparent, continuous monitoring using CSA STAR methodologies greatly enhances proactive risk management, making security breaches less likely and minimizing their potential impact.

Our pilots involve collaboration with well-known CSPs, GRC product vendors, and authorized 3PAOs.

See our accompanying [blog post](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/blog/2025/07/02/strategic-synergy-csa-star-ccm-and-fedramp-20x).

Please reach out to me if you are interested in learning more.
