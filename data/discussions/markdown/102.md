## pete-gov (FedRAMP) — [2025-11-28T14:32:21Z](https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/102#discussioncomment-15106302)

> 1. To clarify FRR-FSI-13, is the "automatic acknowledgement" a reply sent back to the email from FedRAMP or is this encouraging CSPs to have automated notification/workflows internally to respond to emails from FedRAMP? We will set up automation internally to note messages that come in and are wondering if FedRAMP is looking for an auto-reply email for FRR-FSI-13. If so, is there any preferred format or content that should be in the automatic reply?

Ah, the intent is for automatic acknowledgement to be sent back to the sender (an email auto-reply is likely the default). This will help us understand other potential issues, such as if the email is received to an inbox but no actions are taken then we can be confident it's a problem with how the cloud serviced provider is triaging messages rather than it being a bad email inbox or similar.

No preferred format/content at this time, though it'd be cool to see rules for auto-reply set up based on the rest of the requirements (that is, respond differently to "Important" vs "Emergency" etc.). We're not expecting people to do that unless they want to though!
