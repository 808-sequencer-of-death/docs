# Aeroplicity - FedRAMP 20x Phase Two Pilot Draft
Source: https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/94

## Original post — jerome-miastkowski, 2025-10-01T18:01:50Z

Aeroplicity is committed to full transparency in our approach to FedRAMP 20x. Our goal is to help strengthen the overall program and give back to the community. It’s been inspiring to see the diverse approaches and contributions from others so far.

We have been actively implementing the requirements as part of the FedRAMP 20x Phase One Pilot. With our implementation of the Moderate KSIs nearly complete, we’re excited about the potential opportunity to participate in the Phase Two Pilot.

You can view our draft public repo @ https://github.com/aero-plicity/fedramp-20x-public

You can also view our live Trust Center @ https://aeroplicity.com/trust-center

A huge thanks to @pete-gov and team for modernizing the FedRAMP Authorization process.
