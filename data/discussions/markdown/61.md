Last month we brought you a [panel discussion with a bunch of 20x Phase One pilot participants](https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/47); this month we've asked two cloud service providers to give everyone a deeper look into their actual workflow to make this stuff work. 

### Please [join us Wednesday, July 23 from 1pm to 2pm](https://gsa.zoomgov.com/meeting/register/FpW3sJuBRxag_1Mz49J0Cw) for another special extended 20x Community Working Group meeting to see demos of the 20x approach in action!

> If you're already registered, make a note of that bonus +30min that won't show up on your calendar! And if you can't make it, no worries, we'll [post it on youtube](https://www.youtube.com/c/fedramp) with all of our other Community Working Group meetings.

## Special Guests & Demos

### [Knox Systems](https://www.knoxsystems.com/)

- **Presenter:** **Chris Johnson**, CTO, Knox Systems (fedramp20x@knoxsystems.com)

  CJ has spent two decades in cloud. The first decade was at Avanade, a Microsoft-Accenture JV, where he pioneered enterprise migrations of MS Office to the cloud. For the past 10 years, he’s built and operated Knox’s federal environment, including Adobe’s FedRAMP and DISA IL4-authorized Federal Cloud.

- **Knox FedRAMP20x Submission:** https://github.com/Knox-Gov/fedramp_20x_pilot
- **KnoxAI Demo:** https://www.loom.com/share/81967387bf12411686e72e9b094ac928?sid=faac753d-d234-4524-b0a9-5bba28d48d87 

**Coalfire (presenting with Knox Systems)**
- **Presenter:** **John Putalavage**, Managing Principal of Cloud Technology Services

### [Meridian Knowledge Solutions](https://meridianks.com/)

- **Presenter:** **Adam Burroughs**, Director of Federal Cybersecurity Operations

  Director of Federal Cybersecurity Operations at Meridian, with over 12 years of experience in IT and a focused track record in FedRAMP security operations. Previously led the Rev. 4 Moderate authorization for Ivanti’s ITSM platform and served as the FedRAMP Technical Program Manager for both Rev. 4 and Rev. 5 authorizations at NetDocuments. At Meridian, leads public sector cybersecurity strategy and execution, including participation in the FedRAMP 20x pilot.

- **Meridian FedRAMP 20x submission:** https://meridian-knowledge-solutions.github.io/fedramp_20x_public_submission-live/

**Fortreum (presenting with Meridian)**
- **Presenter: Ethan Troy**, Senior Consultant
