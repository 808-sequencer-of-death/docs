You can expect us to spam pretty much every channel we have to warn people about this, it will be hard to miss. :)

To ensure you're in the loop on public notifications from FedRAMP, you should:

1. Monitor the fedramp.gov/changelog and fedramp blog
2. Subscribe to email notifications from FedRAMP via our [GovDelivery](https://public.govdelivery.com/accounts/USGSA/subscriber/new)
3. Follow FedRAMP on social media (links at the bottom of fedramp.gov)
4. Continue to monitor the FedRAMP Community in this GitHub discussion forum
