# Machine-readable intake of CISA BODs?
Source: https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/90

## Original post — iteuscher, 2025-09-24T19:16:57Z

In the new [FedRAMP Vulnerability Detection and Response Standard](https://github.com/FedRAMP/docs/blob/main/markdown/FRMR.VDR.vulnerability-detection-and-response.md#frr-vdr-tf-02) it calls out remediating Known Exploited Vulnerabilities as required by CISA Binding Operational Directive (BOD) 22-01. I know CISA publishes KEV data in a machine-readable, easy to ingest format (JSON here: https://github.com/cisagov/kev-data and API available)

Is anyone aware of a similar place or process to ingest CISA BODs other than web scraping the HTML pages here: https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/directives ?

Curious if anyone has a way to automate bringing in BODs or if CISA is planning to publish their BODs in a similar machine-readable format to the KEVs and [CSAF advisories](https://github.com/cisagov/CSAF/tree/develop) they put out. 

While we are bringing in machine-readable evidence, vulnerability data, and FedRAMP 20x requirements it would be nice to bring in the CISA BODs as well. As far as I'm aware there isn't an explicit FedRAMP requirement for CSPs to meet all CISA BODs but they are mandatory for agencies... and a good idea for everyone else 

-- Isaac Teuscher (Paramify)
