> **pete-gov (FedRAMP)** — [2025-07-16T12:18:03Z](https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/55#discussioncomment-13776638)
>
> All of these timelines and answers are subject to change - this isn't business advice, please don't make business decisions based on our transparency with goals, etc. etc. etc.
> 
> 1. The plan is for Phase Two to be open to the public, but folks who actively participated in Phase One will be _prioritized._ That may or may not matter depending on demand, but our team's capacity is still small and the pilots include some pretty bespoke artisanal hand crafting that take time and effort to unravel. If everyone that participated in the Phase One pilot submits during Phase Two then there's a good chance that will fill up our capacity in Phase Two.
> 
> 2. Pilots are not ideal for companies who aren't willing to take risks and commit to work that will likely need to be redone later. Generally, if a company is trying to optimize their investment and involvement, they are likely much better off waiting for a formal path that has a few more guardrails and for _automation tooling to be more widely available_ so folks don't have to roll their own.
> 
> Our intent is that anyone doing work against the 20x Phase One requirements will be well positioned to obtain a formal 20x Low authorization when that path is released (and repeat, for Phase Two).
