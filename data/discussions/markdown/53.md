## pete-gov (FedRAMP) — [2025-07-02T15:01:13Z](https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/53#discussioncomment-13642238)

> 1. Are 3PAO personnel still expected to complete the 3PAO quiz?

For the moment, FedRAMP hasn't made any official changes to expectations here for Rev5 even though those materials are a bit out of date. That said, 3PAOs are expected to manage their own training of personnel and FedRAMP is not verifying that these trainings are happening.
 
> 2. Are there still plans to create training for Federal agency personnel either for Rev. 5 or FedRAMP 20x (Did not see it on the roadmap)?

There are no plans for this throughout the rest of FY25 and FY26; it's included in the "nearly all other previously discussed work" that has been stopped in the [FedRAMP in 2025 announcement](https://www.fedramp.gov/2025-03-24-FedRAMP-in-2025/). Providing training is not a statutory requirement for FedRAMP and is minimized as an responsibility in M-24-15 that has been deferred as a non-critical priority for the program currently.
