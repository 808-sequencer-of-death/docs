## pete-gov (FedRAMP) — [2025-07-31T21:12:38Z](https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/67#discussioncomment-13952034)

> When reviewing the controls with our 3PAO partner, it appears that a great many of the controls are handled by the SaaS platforms we leverage. 

Your provider in this case is a third-party information resource and you will inherit the controls from them. Please note that for the 20xP1 pilot underlying infrastructure must already be FedRAMP Authorized.

The [Minimum Assessment Standard](https://github.com/FedRAMP/docs/blob/main/markdown/FRMR.MAS.minimum-assessment-standard.md) outlines the expected approach. 

_edit to add:_ If you're talking about just third-party providers in general (not the underlying infrastructure) then there's a bit more flexibility but again, the MAS provides the guidance here.
