# AU-11 retention period
Source: https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/105

## Original post — cbiggsrun, 2025-12-17T20:10:31Z

[Someone posted on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/petewaterman_because-fedramp-didnt-release-much-detail-activity-7405575417782292481-xurB/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAABw_Rr4Bo25cAgpDBd9FZZxepAzwf3xIN9Y) that they did a comparison of the updated Rev 5 SRTM and SSP templates and found a few small but meaningful changes, most notably the clarification on AU-11 retention period. The bottom line is if you can enable an API for customers to pull their own logs and store them themselves, you won’t have to abide by the M-21-31 retention period of 12 months online then 18 months offline for customer audit records because it’s now a customer responsibility. 

Bloody brilliant idea, however, the question now is how long does a CSP have to archive their own production environment security logs offline. AU-11 says you have to keep 90 days (3 months) online, but offline should follow NARA. Welp, [NARA (GRS 3.2)](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/grs/grs03-2.pdf) Item 036, Cybersecurity logging records, says to retain them for a total of 30 months, which means we're back to retaining environment logs for a total of 2.5 years, which is a long time for large CSPs. Or does NARA Item 036 apply only to agency audit records and not CSPs? Can CSPs decide on their own retention period for archived logs?

## pete-gov (FedRAMP) — [2025-12-17T22:27:12Z](https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/105#discussioncomment-15283747)

The "offline should follow NARA" comment in AU-11 would **only** apply to federal agencies implementing this control; the General Records Schedule absolutely does not apply to non-federal entities. Non-federal entities should not pay attention to that portion of the supplemental guidance (or the part about FOIA). In general, please do not try to apply federal requirements to non-federal entities. ;) 

>  Can CSPs decide on their own retention period for archived logs?

Exactly. The organization (aka the CSP) should define the time period, consistent with their policies related to auditing, investigation, etc.
