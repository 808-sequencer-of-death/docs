# Announcing two Rev5-focused Continuous Vulnerability Management special events!
Source: https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/62

## Original post — pete-gov (FedRAMP), 2025-07-21T18:23:16Z

Last week we released [RFC-0012, a proposed FedRAMP Continuous Vulnerability Management Standard](https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/59). Similar to the [Significant Change Notification Standard](https://www.fedramp.gov/20x/standards/) for 20x, this standard is a significant shift fueled by the expectations in [M-24-15](https://www.fedramp.gov/assets/resources/documents/FedRAMP_Policy_Memo.pdf). After public comment, this standard will be carefully and deliberately tested and evaluated with Rev5 cloud service providers in both closed and open betas prior to wide release.

FedRAMP is hosting two events to spur conversation around the changes in RFC-0012 and encourage formal public comment, with both events focused primarily on Rev5-based FedRAMP authorizations and currently authorized cloud service providers.

---

## Rev5 Community Working Group Special Event w/ the FedRAMP TAG

**When:** Wednesday, August 6, from 1pm to 2pm Eastern
**Sign Up:** Register for the [recurring Rev5 Community Working Group meeting here](https://www.fedramp.gov/community/) (if you're already signed up then you're good to go!).

The FedRAMP Technical Advisory Group (TAG) is a core part of FedRAMP's success. At this session, you'll hear from some of the TAG members about vulnerability management in government, why this updated standard is important, and in some cases where they disagree or think it fell short. We'll also host open Q&A with the audience about Rev5-related topics and share an update on the Significant Change Notification Closed Beta progress.
