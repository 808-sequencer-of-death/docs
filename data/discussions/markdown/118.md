## pete-gov (FedRAMP) — [2026-01-16T22:21:34Z](https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/118#discussioncomment-15522024)

BLUF: No, FedRAMP is something different, but you should actually ask the DOD because it's their rule.

Three things here:

**(1) FedRAMP does not set CMMC requirements, so FedRAMP can't answer what will meet CMMC requirements.** 

I'll note that the [Dec 21, 2023 FedRAMP Equivalency Memo from the DOD CIO](https://dodcio.defense.gov/Portals/0/Documents/Library/FEDRAMP-EquivalencyCloudServiceProviders.pdf) includes additional information on what is expected of FedRAMP Moderate Equivalency, including a bunch of materials that **not** part of the 20x process. There appears to be no way for someone following the 20x process to meet FRME as explained in that memo.

**(2) FedRAMP is no longer implemented in the law or by policy in a way that aligns with the expectations of DFARS clause 252.204-7012.**

FedRAMP 20x is an initiative to match the modern legal implementation of FedRAMP which is an assessment and authorization process that ensures sufficient materials are available for an authorizing official to make a decision. It is not equivalent to an ATO on its own and is not designed to prove that certain security measures are in place/etc. It likely would not make sense for the DOD to adopt FedRAMP 20x equivalency for CMMC without also adding someone responsible for deciding if the implementation documented by the 20x equivalent process demonstrates sufficient security.

**(3) FedRAMP is explicitly for cloud services used by federal agencies.** CMMC is entirely out of scope under the law and is a totally different thing, as CMMC is for businesses that are not federal agencies.
