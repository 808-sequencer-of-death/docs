## pete-gov (FedRAMP) — [2026-11-09T16:20:35Z](https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/195#discussioncomment-19496598)

> What is the UEI?

Ah, this isn't a FedRAMP/infosec thing, it's a government procurement thing.

The Unique Entity ID is a 12 character alphanumeric code [assigned by GSA through SAM.gov](https://sam.gov/entity-registration) to identify companies that do business with the government. It's not required for FedRAMP Certification but is a core part of actually doing any procurement with the government. 

Additional information about the UEI can be found at the [Federal Service Desk](https://www.fsd.gov/gsafsd_sp/en/entity-registration-what-s-the-difference-between-only-getting-a-unique?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0051214) here.
