## pete-gov (FedRAMP) — [2025-08-27T18:59:25Z](https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/80#discussioncomment-14236638)

A minor trigger note: FedRAMP doesn't give out ATOs, we give out "authorizations" which are a certification that someone has passed a FedRAMP assessment as determined by FedRAMP. These authorizations are then used by agencies to create an ATO for a system that uses a FedRAMP authorized CSO and/or to assess CSOs to integrate into existing ATOs.

That clarification made up front:

1. All 20x pilot authorizations are "program authorizations" meaning the assessment, review, and certification for FedRAMP authorization is done entirely by the FedRAMP PMO. No agency sponsor is needed.

2. The designation will be "20x Moderate" initially - we also have a marketplace overhaul on our roadmap, but frankly the current underlying spreadsheet and sync process that is used to create the marketplace data doesn't support a more nuanced approach. In other words, this is the way we could make it work without re-engineering a bunch of stuff that we're hoping to replace soon regardless. ;) At some point in the future the authorization path (20x vs Rev5) will be a separate designation.

Also being honest, I expect initially some people will see 20x as "less" - but I expect this will at some point in the near future flip and folks will see Rev5 that way instead.
