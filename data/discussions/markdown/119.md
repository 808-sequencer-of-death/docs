## mgcas-roxanne — [2026-01-28T00:27:17Z](https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/119#discussioncomment-15622899)

We have recently reviewed the POA&M Template from 2025-12-05 and also request clarification on why "**Service Name**" was removed when its purpose is to specify which exact service(s) are affected for each POA&M ID row? 

Additionally, We have noted the following changes:

Added

- Guidance column added in column A of the "Open POA&M Items" tab.
- Li-SaaS is an option now in the impact level cell (D3) of the "Open POA&M Items" tab
- New "PL-2 Findings" tab


Changed

- **DISCREPANCY**: "Overall Remediation Plan" updated to "Remediation Plan" in the "Open POA&M Items" tab; however, it remained "Overall Remediation Plan" in the "Closed POA&M Items" and "Configuration Findings" tabs.
	

Removed from "Open POA&M Items", "Closed POA&M Items" and "Configuration Findings" tab:

- The CSP info in rows 1-3 
- Planned Milestones
- Milestone Changes
- Auto-Approve
- Service Name


## pete-gov (FedRAMP) — [2026-01-29T15:25:54Z](https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/119#discussioncomment-15641460)

I'm trying to track down more info on this; the changes resulted from multiple years of feedback gathering and some of the background appears to have been lost with some of our staff. Lots of folks apparently found that column very confusing and it created more issues than it helped.

In the interim I'll say that the template contains the minimum expected information; if a cloud service provider has a good reason to include additional information then they should do so. No one should feel like removing this from the template means they aren't allowed to track and report on it if they found it useful.

 Coming back on this to close this question out - this column was inadvertently added originally, did not have lots of guidance and support, and was thus removed. FR does not have any issue with folks adding it back in if a particular CSP wants to or an agency customer wants it from them.

> **paulagosta (FedRAMP)** — [2026-03-11T11:58:22Z](https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/119#discussioncomment-16082658)
>
> Blake,
> 
> The concept was not removed, just the explicit field.  It was removed as we found a lot of redundancy of information between that and the remediation plan fields across the POA&Ms we examined.  
> 
> If you look at the template itself, each column includes three rows: one with general guidance, one with examples, and one indicating whether the field is mandatory or situational. 
> 
> In the general guidance it states "Describe the plan to resolve the weakness, especially if it cannot be fully remediated within the required timeframe. Provide enough detail to help agency customers understand the actions required, why more time is needed, and when the weakness will be fully remediated."
> 
> In the example it explicitly states milestones. 
> 
> "Will be resolved during <date> patching cycle.
> 
> OR
> 
> Migrate to new technical solution. Planned milestones are:
> 1) Milestone 1
> 2) Milestone 2
> 3) …..."
> 
> Hope this helps. 
> 
> Pablo
