The _Notes_ in the rules are undefeated. My favorite is the one in CMU-CSO-TSC: "Adding a non-validated algorithm while a CMVP-validated module still protects federal customer data, such as hybrid implementation, is not such a change." Allows a CSP to add a PQC-approved algo. as an option to the algo that was validated as part of the module without going through a transformative.

> **pete-gov (FedRAMP)** — [2026-10-21T14:41:22Z](https://github.com/FedRAMP/community/discussions/181#discussioncomment-19099909)
>
> Hmmm, just to clarify, that note means you can use a CMVP-validated module to protect information _and then wrap it in a PQC resistant algo that isn't CMVP-validated_ (or vice versa) and that's likely not a transformative change (and it's good security). This hybrid implementation can be a bit expensive but it's the most effective way to dot the i's and cross the t's for any customers that are quite picky about sticking with CMVP-validated modules.
